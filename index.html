PARALLEL SYNTHESIS CHAINS 3 CHAINS × 3 ENGINES · ONE MASTER OUTPUT BUS

144 INDEPENDENT PARTS / THREE CHAIN SUMS / MASTER OUTPUT

Each chain contains three complete 16-part synth engines (48 parts per chain). Each engine retains its FM, multisample, modulation, insert effects and sequencer architecture. The three engines sum into their own chain gain stage. All three chains then sum into one shared effects and master output bus.
